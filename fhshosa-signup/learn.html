So... what is HOSA?

MORE THAN A HEALTH CLUB.

HOSA–Future Health Professionals is a student-led organization that helps students explore the health field through education, leadership, service, and hands-on experiences. You don't have to have your whole career figured out to be curious.

A few quick answers

GOOD TO KNOW.

Do I need to know exactly what healthcare career I want?

No. HOSA is a way to explore the healthcare field. Being curious is a great starting point.

Is HOSA only for future doctors or nurses?

Not at all. Healthcare has many paths—including laboratory science, public health, therapy, technology, administration, and more.

What do HOSA members actually do?

Depending on the chapter and school year, HOSA members can learn about health careers, develop leadership skills, join service activities, and participate in competitions. Follow FHS HOSA for chapter-specific updates.

Where can I find official competition information?

Use the Texas HOSA link above for state-level information, or visit hosa.org for international event guidelines and resources. Your school chapter can help explain which opportunities apply.
